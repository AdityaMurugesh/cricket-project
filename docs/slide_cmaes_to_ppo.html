How CMA-ES connects to PPO: a warm start, not a reward term

CMA-ES finds one fast, legal delivery. Its torques are copied into PPO's own network, and PPO carries on training with the normal task reward.
NOT USEDImitation reward: add a term that pays the policy for staying close to the reference trajectory.
USEDInitialise the policy from the reference: behaviour cloning, then PPO with the unchanged task reward.
Step 1: optimise

CMA-ES

scripts/kin_frontier.py
  • Searches 238 numbers: 7 arm motors × 34 knots, one every 20 ms from the hand-over (0.5 s before release).
  • Fitness: -speed if the ball is legal (elbow ≤ 15°), on a good length and line, and good often enough under timing error; otherwise a penalty that grows with the violation.
  • Release angle fixed per search (270° / 290°); the release time follows from the motion.
Output: one open-loop torque plan, e.g. 130 km/h, legal, on a good length.
Step 2: copy

Behaviour cloning

scripts/bc_from_frontier.py
  • Bowl the plan from 41 start times, 8 times each, with small random action noise, so the data also covers states near the plan.
  • Label every state with the plan's torque at that moment.
  • Actor (PPO's policy network) trained to output those torques (mean-squared error). Critic trained on each rollout's delivery reward. Exploration noise set small.
Output: a PPO network that already bowls the CMA-ES delivery.
Step 3: improve

PPO fine-tuning

scripts/train_runup.py --resume-from
  • The same network keeps training with the normal task reward (_task_reward): good ball → 1 + 0.5 × speed (m/s); miss → −distance; illegal → −distance − 2; all × 10.
  • For a timing skill, the reward is the expected value over release-timing error.
  • No term rewards staying close to the CMA-ES plan: PPO is free to move away from it.
Output: a feedback policy: it sees the arm every 20 ms and can correct.
What CMA-ES alone cannot do: its plan is open loop (the same torques whatever happens). Release-timing error alone hits a plan and a policy alike. Error during the swing (motor noise) can only be corrected by a policy.
Same-noise test (running on the HPC): CMA-ES plan vs its clone vs clone + PPO, all scored under identical timing error and motor noise (scripts/compare_cmaes_rl.py).
